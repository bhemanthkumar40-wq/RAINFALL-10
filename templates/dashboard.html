About This Prediction & Limitations

Dataset Provenance: Derived from official Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) historical records (1901–2015, Kaggle rajanand/rainfall-in-india) covering 115 continuous years across all 36 subdivisions.

Model Calibration: Evaluated on 9,879 held-out test months with zero data leakage. The classification model uses a composite F1-score (0.896) and ROC-AUC (0.949) to counter seasonal class imbalances.

Notice: Probabilities represent statistical model estimates based on historical atmospheric patterns, not absolute meteorological guarantees. Local micro-climates, sudden depressions, and cyclone tracks may alter real-time conditions.
