Evaluation Methodology & Class Balance Rationale

Why Accuracy Alone is Insufficient: In meteorological time series, precipitation exhibits pronounced seasonal asymmetry—off-monsoon winter months are consistently dry, while monsoon months are intensely wet. If a model trivially predicted "No Rain" during dry seasons or "Rain" during monsoon peaks, it could register high raw accuracy while failing completely when seasonal transitions occur.

Decision Metric: The selected model is determined by optimizing the harmonic balance of F1-Score (the harmonic mean of Precision and Recall) and the Receiver Operating Characteristic Area Under Curve (ROC-AUC). This guarantees that the system minimizes false positives (false alarms for emergency planners) while preventing dangerous false negatives (unpredicted severe rainfall events).

Validation Integrity: Features are normalized using StandardScaler and categorical subdivisions encoded using OneHotEncoder wrapped strictly inside an isolated Scikit-Learn Pipeline. Transformers are fitted exclusively on the 80% training split, completely preventing test data contamination.
