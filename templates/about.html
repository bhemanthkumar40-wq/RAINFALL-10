Rainfall Intelligence is an empirical meteorological analysis and predictive machine learning platform designed to address critical weather forecasting, agricultural scheduling, and flood disaster preparedness challenges across the Indian subcontinent. Developed by Hemanth Kumar, this system replaces traditional heuristic weather calculations with 7 transparent machine learning regressors evaluated on transparent, held-out splits.

IMD 115-Year Climate Data Characteristics

Historical observational data published by the Indian Meteorological Department (1901–2015)

Kaggle Verified

Key Dataset Dimensions

  • Temporal Span: 115 consecutive meteorological calendar years (1901 to 2015).
  • Geographical Subdivisions: All 36 official IMD agro-climatic zones across mainland India and union territories.
  • Monthly Granularity: Independent rainfall observations for all 12 calendar months (JAN through DEC).
  • Seasonal Aggregations: Four seasonal rainfall totals:
    • Jan–Feb: Winter Non-Monsoon Season
    • Mar–May: Pre-Monsoon Summer / Thunderstorm Season
    • Jun–Sep: Southwest Summer Monsoon (75% of national precipitation)
    • Oct–Dec: Northeast Post-Monsoon / Retreating Monsoon
